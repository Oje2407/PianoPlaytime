## Full description (both stores)
Little Keys turns your phone or tablet into a colourful piano that teaches children to play.

Every note has its own colour and animal friend: C for Cat, D for Dog, E for Elephant, and so on. Glowing keys show your child exactly what to press next, so they can play real songs from the very first day.

LEARN STEP BY STEP
Five short lessons take children from finding the black-key "twins" to playing their first scale. They learn middle C, five-finger position and finger numbers along the way.

PLAY REAL SONGS
Hot Cross Buns, Mary Had a Little Lamb, Ode to Joy, Twinkle Twinkle Little Star and Jingle Bells. Tap "Listen first" to hear the tune, then follow the glowing keys and the words.

GAMES THAT TEACH
• Note Detective: a letter appears, find its key.
• Copy Cat: listen to a tune and play it back. It grows a little longer each time.

FREE PLAY
Make up your own music with piano or music-box sounds.

EARN STARS
Up to three stars for every lesson, song and game.

MADE FOR CHILDREN, TRUSTED BY PARENTS

• No accounts or sign-in
• No data collected
• Works completely offline
• No links out of the app

Little Keys is screen time that builds a real skill. Pay once and every lesson, song and game is yours, with nothing more to buy.
