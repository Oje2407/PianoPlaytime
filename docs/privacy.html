Little Keys Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Little Keys is a piano learning app for children. We built it to collect nothing.

What we collect

Nothing. Little Keys does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information from children or adults. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no photos, no microphone use and no location use.

Advertising and tracking

Little Keys has no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party code that collects data.

Internet access

The app works fully offline and does not send anything over the internet. The only link out of the app is to this privacy policy, inside the grown-ups' settings, which are protected by an adult check.

Saved on the device

Stars, lesson progress and settings are saved only on the device, inside the app. If a grown-up chooses to add the child's age or a first name for the certificate, these are also saved only on the device. We cannot see any of this. Deleting the app, or choosing Reset progress in Settings, removes it.

Purchases

Little Keys is free and has no in-app purchases.

Children's privacy

Because we collect no personal information, the app is designed to meet the requirements of the UK Age Appropriate Design Code, UK GDPR, the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the Apple App Store Kids category and the Google Play Families policy.

Changes

If this ever changes, we will update this page and the date above before releasing the new version.
