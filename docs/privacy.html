Little Keys Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Little Keys is a piano learning app for children. We built it to collect nothing.

What we collect

Nothing. Little Keys does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information from children or adults. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no names, no photos, no microphone use and no location use.

Advertising and tracking

Little Keys has no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party code that collects data.

Internet access

The app works fully offline. It does not send anything over the internet, and it contains no links to websites, social media or other apps.

Stars and progress

The stars your child earns are saved only on the device, inside the app. We cannot see them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Purchases

Little Keys has no in-app purchases.

Children's privacy

Because we collect no personal information, the app is designed to meet the requirements of the UK Age Appropriate Design Code, UK GDPR, the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the Apple App Store Kids category and the Google Play Families policy.

Changes

If this ever changes, we will update this page and the date above before releasing the new version.
